Work Unit of the Secretariat General of DPR RI

Travel Division
Secretariat General of DPR RI

The Travel Division is a work unit under the Bureau of Finance, led by the Head of the Travel Division, and is responsible for administering and coordinating domestic and international official travel for Members of DPR RI and employees of the Secretariat General of DPR RI.

Stage 01 · Assignment

Assignment Administration through ALADIN

The process begins with assignment administration. ALADIN supports the preparation and management of official travel assignment letters as the administrative basis for subsequent travel processes.

Stage output: orderly and traceable assignment documents and data for use in the travel-submission process.

Stage 02 · Service Submission

PANDORA and SiMerpati

At the submission stage, digital services are tailored to user groups. PANDORA supports self-service travel submissions, while SiMerpati supports official travel services for Members of DPR RI and Expert Staff.

Stage objective: speed up service, reduce manual processing, and improve status traceability.

Stage 03 · Accountability

Post-Travel Documentation

After travel is completed, accountability documents are submitted and managed digitally so travel evidence, expenditure records, and supporting documents are more orderly and traceable.

Stage objective: support documented and accountable post-travel administrative settlement.

Stage 04 · Verification

Review through the Verifier Dashboard

Accountability documents enter the review and verification process by Travel Division verifiers. The dashboard helps display document status, process aging, delays, revisions, and priority files.

Stage objective: identify priority documents faster and support more focused verification work.

Stage 05 · Control

Follow-up on Verification Results

Verification results are followed up according to document conditions, including corrections, revisions, returns, completion of deficiencies, or other administrative actions. Follow-up status is updated for monitoring.

Stage objective: ensure every issue or correction requirement has a clear and documented follow-up.

Stage 06 · Monitoring

Monitoring through the Executive Dashboard

Official travel developments are presented in a management dashboard to help leaders monitor conditions across work units in real time.

Stage objective: provide concise status, trend, and key-indicator information to support monitoring and decision-making.

Assignment Stage

ALADIN

ALADIN supports the administration and preparation of official travel assignment letters. The assignment letter serves as the administrative basis that initiates the official travel process before service submission and implementation.

Digitization at this stage supports more orderly, traceable, and well-documented assignment administration.

Main role:
• preparing and managing assignment letters;
• documenting assignment data;
• supporting continuity into subsequent travel processes.

Open Official Travel Application
Self-Service Submission

PANDORA

PANDORA is a digital official-travel service supporting independent submissions so administrative processes can be handled more quickly, responsively, and with traceable records.

The system reduces reliance on manual processes, makes service status easier to monitor, and supports greater transparency in official-travel submissions.

Main role:
• digital official-travel submission;
• process and status monitoring;
• submission-history documentation.

Members & Expert Staff Service

SiMerpati

SiMerpati is an information system supporting official travel management for Members of DPR RI and Expert Staff. It simplifies administration and makes travel-process monitoring easier through digital services.

Main role:
• supporting travel administration for Members and Expert Staff;
• simplifying service processes and documents;
• improving process-status visibility.

Post-Travel

Official Travel Accountability Application

This application supports the submission and management of accountability documents after official travel is completed. Its focus is to make post-travel administration more orderly, documented, and traceable.

Main role:
• digital submission of accountability documents;
• management of supporting evidence and administrative completeness;
• tracking the accountability settlement process.

Open System
Verification & Control

Verifier Dashboard

The Verifier Dashboard supports Travel Division verifiers in reviewing, correcting, verifying, controlling, and following up official travel accountability documents.

It presents information that helps prioritize work, including document status, process aging, delays, revisions, returns, and documented follow-up.

Key value:
• faster identification of priority files;
• improved control of workload and process age;
• stronger follow-up documentation;
• monitoring information for management.

Management Monitoring

Executive Dashboard

The Executive Dashboard presents real-time official travel developments across work units in a concise management-information format.

It helps leaders quickly view official-travel conditions, monitor developments between units, recognize major trends and statuses, and access supporting information for monitoring and decision-making.

Key value:
• work-unit official-travel monitoring;
• real-time presentation of indicators and status;
• information support for management control and decision-making.
